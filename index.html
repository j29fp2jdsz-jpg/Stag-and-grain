Furniture restoration

Give good furniture a new chapter.

We restore a wide range of well-made furniture — not only Stag. Whether it needs repair, preparation, refinishing or a complete visual transformation, the starting point is simple: show us what you have and tell us what you want it to become.

1Send a few clear photographs and a short description of the piece.

3We’ll give you a clear quotation before any work begins.

Our work

Classic furniture, reimagined with restraint.

The goal is not to hide what the furniture is. It is to preserve the features worth keeping, restore the parts that need attention and give the whole piece a finish that feels right for its next home.

About Stag & Grain

Two old friends. Two skilled trades. One shared obsession.

Stag & Grain brings together the hands of a master carpenter and the eye of an experienced decorator. The idea is simple: good furniture deserves better than being discarded.

Stag furniture became a natural speciality because of how well it was made and how beautifully it responds to careful restoration. But the same approach applies to any quality piece with good bones, character and years left in it.
